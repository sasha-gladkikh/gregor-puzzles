# Conditions and evidence

Ten distinct gene–condition pairs were selected from the supplied GFT subset to represent different biological processes. Rounds retain the relative order of that subset. Selection reflects teaching variety, not clinical priority. Repeated rows do not create additional rounds.

## The ten rounds

1. **CLCN1** · [Recessive myotonia congenita](https://omim.org/entry/255700). Skeletal muscle chloride conductance.
2. **GJB2** · [Autosomal recessive nonsyndromic hearing loss 1A](https://omim.org/entry/220290). Gap-junction communication.
3. **SLC29A3** · [Histiocytosis-lymphadenopathy plus syndrome](https://omim.org/entry/602782). Lysosomal nucleoside transport.
4. **SBDS** · [Shwachman-Diamond syndrome 1](https://omim.org/entry/260400). Ribosome maturation.
5. **RNU4-2** · [ReNU syndrome](https://omim.org/entry/620851). A functional RNA in the major spliceosome.
6. **CACNA1A** · [Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 42](https://omim.org/entry/617106). Calcium channels and synaptic release.
7. **PTPN11** · [Noonan syndrome 1](https://omim.org/entry/163950). SHP2 and RAS/MAPK regulation.
8. **RNU4ATAC** · [Roifman syndrome](https://omim.org/entry/616651). Minor-intron splicing.
9. **STING1** · [STING-associated vasculopathy with onset in infancy](https://omim.org/entry/615934). Excess interferon signaling.
10. **FGF14** · [Spinocerebellar ataxia 27B](https://omim.org/entry/620174). An intronic GAA repeat expansion.

FAM177A1 was discussed during planning but was absent from the supplied pairs. The [earlier selection rationale](selection-rationale.pdf) records the original design; the web review contains the expanded clinical edition.

## Educational content

Each round includes a learning objective, a clinical pattern, selected findings, molecular interpretation notes, and one connection to remember. Completing all 12 pieces finishes the round; there are no quizzes. During play, clues unlock at 2, 4, 6, and 8 pieces (location, function, clinical pattern, inheritance), and each of three image notes appears when the piece containing it is placed. The completion card shows the gene, condition, mechanism, clinical pattern, onset and course, clinical significance, and family implications. Science contains the full profile and variant interpretation; Editorial review previews player-facing wording. Gene recognition is optional. Unambiguous product names complete the image and teach the corresponding symbol; shared RNA names such as U4 prompt for a specific locus. A reusable symbol hint becomes available after eight pieces.

Rounds 05 (U4 release at activation), 07 (SHP-2 autoinhibition), and 09 (STING activation without cGAMP) include a hand-drawn before/after mechanism figure. Rounds 05 and 08 compare the major and minor spliceosomes. These additions, the SAVI caveat on interferon-independent disease in mouse models, the Rafehi et al. co-discovery reference for FGF14, and the Hsu et al. reference for ENT3 require review like all other content.

The 79 clinical profile entries are grouped into characteristic patterns, additional or variable findings, and course. They include modifiers and typical negative findings, not 79 independent symptoms. The 50 HPO terms provide vocabulary mappings, not formal disease annotations. Teaching patterns do not establish diagnostic specificity or phenotype frequency.

## Sources and interpretation

References accompany each round in **Science** and **Editorial review** and in [rounds.mjs](../game/rounds.mjs). Scientific sources were checked on **15 October 2026**. Clinical summaries paraphrase GeneReviews, MedlinePlus Genetics, and primary research. OMIM links identify the corresponding condition; the prose is not an extraction of OMIM entries.

Each mechanism and clinical takeaway has explicit claim sources. Shared sources appear once in a numbered bibliography. Family implications distinguish conditional recurrence, transmission, penetrance, and severity; diagnostic interpretation explains phase, uncertain variants, and assay limits.

Inheritance and variant mechanisms refer to the selected gene–condition pair. They should not be generalized to every association of that gene. The content distinguishes functional RNAs from proteins, dominant from recessive associations, and repeat expansions from coding variants.

Illustrations are AI-generated conceptual models, not atomic structures, measured traces, complete pathways, or diagnostic thresholds. [Illustration notes](../game/artwork-data.mjs) contain landmark explanations and supporting references; [generation prompts](../game/artwork/prompts.json) record provenance.

## Review and data scope

**All ten rounds require scientific review.** Educational effectiveness and retention have not been evaluated. This is a research education prototype, not a clinical decision tool. Use the [reviewer guide](REVIEW.md) to assess each round.

The supplied subset contained pathogenic or likely pathogenic classifications but no site column, so Stanford site membership could not be independently verified from that input. The game includes gene–condition relationships and published biology. It excludes workbook files, family identifiers, individual variants, patient phenotypes, and row-level classifications. Variant descriptions are general published classes.

Community links provide resources without soliciting donations. Branding uses the GSS blue palette and the GREGoR Puzzles DNA–puzzle mark.
