     GETTING STARTED

    How to play

    Assemble the illustration to uncover a gene and its associated condition.

    1. Place the pieces. Drag a piece onto the board. Or select a piece, then click its place.
    2. Connect the clues. Answer a question at six and eight pieces to connect function and inheritance. Read the explanation, then continue.
    3. Identify the gene. Know the gene? Enter its symbol to complete the picture, then finish the learning questions.

    Keyboard: Tab to a piece and press Enter. Tab to a board position and press Enter to place it. Escape clears a selection. Hints and the reference image are available for the image puzzle.

    Your collection: Choose any of the 10 rounds. Progress is stored separately for each round in this browser. Restart affects only the current round.

    Timed mode: elapsed time starts with your first puzzle action and continues through reloads, background tabs, and instructions. It ends when the picture and both questions are complete. Results list your route and assistance; there is no shared ranking.

    Restart this round?

    Restarting clears your pieces, learning answers, and timer. You can choose Practice or Timed again.
