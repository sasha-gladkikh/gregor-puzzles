About

Assemble scientific illustrations to connect rare disease genes, molecular mechanisms, and clinical patterns. Each round explores an association selected from GREGoR Stanford Site findings.

Scientific content

Illustrations are teaching schematics, and clinical descriptions summarize public literature. The game contains no individual patient case records.

Explore mechanisms, clinical features, and references in Science. For scientific reviewers, the review page collects the wording used in each round.

The project

Developed by Sasha Gladkikh in collaboration with Dr. Matthew Wheeler for genetics education. Learn more about the research at the GREGoR Stanford Site.

Suggest a gene–disease association and its clinical features without signing in. Name and email are optional; responses go privately to the team. For corrections or technical issues, use GitHub issues.

For educators and collaborators

Explore GREGoR Puzzles as a teaching activity for genetics and genetic counseling trainees. We welcome conversations about classroom pilots, new scientific content, and educational evaluation.

Contact the GREGoR Stanford Site about classroom use or collaboration.
