About the project

Piece by piece, biology comes into view.

GREGoR Puzzles connects molecular mechanisms with rare disease through illustrated puzzles.

Built for discovery

Each round explores one gene–condition association selected from the GREGoR Stanford Site findings. As you assemble the illustration, clues introduce the biology. The gene and condition are the final reveals.

After completing a round, explore the figure, connect its mechanism to the clinical pattern, and follow the references. The collection covers ten associations across processes including ion transport, RNA splicing, and cell signalling.

Science you can inspect

The game uses public molecular and clinical knowledge. Its illustrations are teaching schematics, and its clinical descriptions summarize published associations. The rounds contain no individual patient case records.

The Science & sources pages provide the clinical context, interpretation notes, and references. The Editorial review page records the content awaiting scientific sign-off.

The project

Developed by Sasha Gladkikh in collaboration with Matthew Wheeler for genetics education and ASHG. Learn more about the research at the GREGoR Stanford Site.

Corrections and teaching suggestions are welcome through GitHub issues. Please use public information only when reporting an issue.
