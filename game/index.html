How times work

Complete all 12 pieces in Timed mode. The clock starts at your first correct placement and continues in the background. Hints and reference views are allowed. Gene-answer completions do not qualify.

Times are self-reported, ranked within each round, and published after review. This measures puzzle speed, not genetics knowledge. GitHub sign-in is needed to submit.
