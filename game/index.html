      How to play

      1. Select a piece. Choose a shape from the tray.
      2. Place it. Tap its position on the board, or drag with a mouse.
      3. Reveal the biology. Build the image to discover the gene and condition.
      Clues and image notes

      Clues appear at 2, 4, 6, and 8 pieces. The gene appears at 11 and the condition at 12. Clues and image notes appear automatically in the list as you place pieces. A brief animation connects each discovery to the image. On a phone, the list is below the puzzle; Clues also opens it without leaving the board. Tap a numbered marker to revisit an image note. Use Gene hint for a biological clue, then a symbol clue if needed. Choose from the Gene bank or type a gene. Gene recognition is optional: a gene symbol or unambiguous product name completes the round early. Hints are available whenever you need them. Finishing the image always reveals the answer.

      Keyboard controls

      Tab to a piece and press Enter. Use the arrow keys to move between board positions and Enter to place it. Escape returns to the tray.

      Practice, timing, and sound

      Practice is the default. Timed mode starts with your first action and keeps counting across reloads and background tabs. Timing measures puzzle play, not clinical knowledge. Sound is on by default; mute it in the header. Your preference is saved.

      Saved progress and shared devices

      Progress stays in this browser. Round settings → Restart clears this round. Browse rounds → New player clears all rounds and restores sound to on.

      Explore completed rounds

      The completed puzzle lights up and gently floats as molecules and selected structures move within the original illustration. Use the control beneath the image to pause or resume. Reduced-motion settings keep the image still. Select Explore the illustration to revisit its notes and sources.
