       GETTING STARTED

      How to play

      Assemble the illustration to uncover a gene and its associated condition.

      1. Place the pieces. Tap or click a piece, then tap or click its place on the board. With a mouse, you can also drag pieces.
      2. Watch the strip under the board. Clues unlock at 2, 4, 6, and 8 pieces; the gene appears at 11 and the condition at 12. Pieces that contain a key part of the biology add a numbered note to the image; tap the number to read it again.
      3. Finish the round. Complete all 12 pieces, or enter the gene symbol if you already know it. The completion card connects mechanism and clinical pattern.

      Keyboard: Tab to a piece and press Enter. Focus moves to the board; use the arrow keys to choose a position and Enter to place it. Escape returns to the pieces.

      Practice and Timed: Practice is the default. Timed mode starts with your first action and continues through reloads and background tabs until the image is complete. Choose before you begin; there is no shared ranking.

      After completion: Select See it work for a guided illustration walkthrough. Play or pause the sequence, or step through at your own pace. Reduce Motion uses manual steps. Quiet placement and completion sounds are on by default. Mute them in the header; your choice is saved.

      Your collection: Progress is stored separately for each round in this browser. Restart affects only the current round. On a shared device, choose New player in All rounds.

      Restart this round?

      Restarting clears your pieces and timer. You can choose Practice or Timed again.

      Start fresh for a new player?

      This clears pieces, hints, and times for every round in this browser and returns to round 01. Editorial review notes are kept.
