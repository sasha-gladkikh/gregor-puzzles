How the leaderboard works

Overall standings count distinct rounds completed. Replaying a round adds an attempt, not another completed round. Players with the same partial progress share a rank. Once every round has a qualifying timed result, the total of their best times breaks ties. Full-set totals reported by players and entered by the project team can also enter the overall standings. The attempt count includes one play per round in a manually entered run, plus recorded plays.

Round standings use your fastest eligible image-puzzle time. Hints and reference views are allowed and shown. Allocated round breakdowns appear separately from timed rankings. Gene-answer completions and untimed practice count toward progress, but not speed rankings. Times are recorded by the browser or reported by players and are not independently verified; they measure puzzle speed, not genetics knowledge.

Join with a display name to save your attempts for the GREGoR Puzzles team and appear publicly. No account required. Use the same browser to keep one player record; matching names on different devices are separate players.
